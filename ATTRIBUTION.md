# Data and media provenance

Inventory metadata and product imagery are captured from the public Cookies Mission Valley WordPress REST interface at build time.

Source site: `https://missionvalley.cookies.co`

The build preserves the source product URL and source identifiers in metadata. Product availability, pricing, potency, descriptions, and imagery remain the responsibility of the source publisher and may change after the frozen snapshot.

No Cookies or Jane trademarks, logos, or product images are claimed as original CANA assets. The frozen media is used to display the source inventory record it accompanies.
